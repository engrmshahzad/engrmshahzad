## 👨‍💻 About Me

I am a passionate and results-driven **Web Applications Specialist** & **Web Developer** at the **U.S.-Pakistan Center for Advanced Studies in Water (USPCAS-W), Mehran University of Engineering & Technology (MUET), Jamshoro**.

With over **14+ years of hands-on experience**, I specialize in architecting, developing, and maintaining high-scale institutional portals, Management Information Systems (MIS), LMS, and custom enterprise web applications.

- 💼 **Current Role:** Web Developer at USPCAS-W, MUET Jamshoro
- 🎓 **Education:** B.E. in Software Engineering (MUET)
- 📜 **Certification:** Oracle 9i Certified Professional (OCP)
- 🎯 **Core Focus:** Scalable Web Applications, Clean UI/UX, Portal Ecosystems, & Database Architecture
- 🌐 **Institutional Profile:** [Faculty Profile @ MUET](https://water.muet.edu.pk/faculty/shahzad-choudhary)
